SisterGo

A women-only two-wheeler taxi platform designed to redefine safety, speed, and empowerment in urban mobility.

About the Platform

SisterGo is a technology-driven mobility platform where only verified female riders provide transportation services exclusively to female passengers using two-wheelers. The platform focuses on safety, affordability, and last-mile connectivity.

Problem Statement

  • • Rising safety concerns for women during daily travel
  • • Limited trusted last-mile transport options
  • • Traffic congestion and time inefficiency
  • • Underrepresentation of women in the mobility workforce

Our Solution

SisterGo introduces a controlled, GPS-enabled, women-only two-wheeler taxi ecosystem that ensures safe travel, faster commute, and structured employment for women.

What SisterGo Does

  • ✔ Connects female passengers with female riders
  • ✔ Provides app-based booking & tracking
  • ✔ Ensures verified & monitored rides
  • ✔ Enables secure digital payments

How the Platform Works

📱 Passenger books ride via app
🛵 Nearby verified female rider assigned
📍 Live GPS tracking & SOS enabled
🏁 Safe drop & digital payment

Platform Benefits

  • ✔ Enhanced women safety
  • ✔ Faster than traditional cabs
  • ✔ Affordable urban transport
  • ✔ Employment generation for women
  • ✔ Supports Smart City & Digital India initiatives
